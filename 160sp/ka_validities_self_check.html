Validity Self-Check — Coming Soon

This page will let you check your own experiment design against Cook and Campbell's four-validities framework: internal, external, construct, and statistical conclusion validity. A structured checklist with feedback on each threat.

Status: Planned — Surface 7b follow-on

The Cook and Campbell content is drafted as a standalone source-of-truth at 160sp/ka_validities_showcase_content_2026-05-18.md. The showcase page (Surface 7b) is the read-side of that content. This page — the self-check — is the active companion where a student can run their own design through the framework.

What the self-check is expected to do:

  • Walk a student through each of the four validities in turn, with the threat list from Shadish, Cook, and Campbell (2002) §2.
  • Ask, for each threat, whether the student's design is exposed and how they intend to mitigate it.
  • Score the design on each validity dimension and surface the weakest links.
  • Cross-reference the substitution skill: if a VR substitution is being made, flag the validity implications of the substitution explicitly (the substitution can trade off one validity dimension for another, and that trade-off should be visible).
  • Save the self-check to the student's profile so it travels with the project across weeks.
