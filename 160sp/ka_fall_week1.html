Annotations — design commentary

For the instructor's review of the Fall journey. Not student-facing copy; sits at the bottom so the page above reads as a student would experience it.

What this surface is

Surface 1 of the nine-surface Week-1 journey: the first page a COGS 160 Fall student reaches after logging in during Week 1. It is the hub from which the three working modes branch. The wireframe source is ka_week1_wireframe_2026-05-17.html §Surface 1.

The phase strip

The eleven-week strip at the top is the orienting affordance — at every moment the student can answer "where am I, how did I get here, what happens next." It is meant to repeat on every Week-N landing. Right now the strip is hard-coded with Week 1 marked current; production should pull the week list from the canonical Fall schedule and mark the current week from calendar integration plus per-student progress tracking, neither of which is wired yet.

The three CTAs

The three entry points are ordered DYK browser, evaluate-a-paper, VR-measurability. CW recommended DYK first as the most engaging entry; the wireframe flagged this as a decision for DK — whether the VR-measurability explainer should come first because a student who has not read it cannot use the other two well. The order here follows the CW recommendation; it is a one-line change if you want VR-measurability first.

The exit-criteria panel

The "By the end of Week 1 you should have" panel is the explicit exit condition. The same four items reappear as a self-check on Surface 9 (the deliverable capture). Making the exit observable is the Track-4 design principle that a journey without a defined exit produces drift.

Dependencies and what is mocked

Week-aware routing (knowing which week the course is in and which week each student should be on) is not wired. The phase strip is hard-coded. The three CTAs link to the other eight surfaces, all of which are themselves new pages built in this same pass. Nothing on this page is wired to the live corpus; the page is structurally complete and content-complete but operationally static until the backend dependencies land.
