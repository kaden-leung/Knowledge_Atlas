Annotations — design commentary

For the instructor's review of the Fall journey. Not student-facing copy; sits at the bottom so the page above reads as a student would experience it.

What this surface is

Surface 2 of the Week-1 journey, rebuilt 2026-05-21 from a “Did-You-Know” surprising-findings browser into a topic browser. The earlier version showed four finding cards and a VOI panel; per DK it was replaced because Fall students do not yet know the field well enough to recognise a topic from a single finding. The surface now shows twenty research-area cards in the home-page card idiom, each with a plain-language hook.

The “More like this” affordance

Each card carries a “More like this” control. Selecting it expands the page in place: a full-width panel of five key-finding cards opens directly beneath the row of the card that was clicked, so the student does not lose their place. Selecting it again collapses the panel; opening a different card's findings closes any panel already open. Six of the twenty topics have their five finding cards written (Nature views, Daylight, Background speech, Ceiling height, Curved vs. angular, Crowding); the remaining fourteen show a short placeholder until their findings are authored.

What is mocked

All twenty topics are real environmental-psychology research areas and the hooks and findings are written from the actual literature, but the page is not yet wired to the live corpus — topic membership, finding counts, and VR-tractability flags are editorial. “Explore this topic” routes every card to the single worked per-topic page (ka_fall_topic.html); production parameterises that page per topic. The family filter is live and client-side.

Open decisions

Whether twenty is the right number on one page or whether the grid should paginate past, say, twelve. Whether the fourteen not-yet-deep topics should be visually marked as lighter-weight, or left identical so the asymmetry is invisible to students. Whether VR-partial topics (daylight, indoor plants, thermal comfort) should be filtered out of Week-1 candidacy or kept with the partial flag, as here.
