Annotations — design commentary

For the instructor's review of the Fall 160 journey redesign. Not student-facing copy; sits at the bottom so the page above reads cleanly as a student would experience it.

What this page is and is not

This is the current student-setup landing for COGS 160. The Fall journey — where students design and then run an experiment — is the design target we are building toward, but the production landing here is the Spring-style track-picker entry that currently lives at ka_student_setup.html. The two are conflated in the production URL; the redesign for Fall has not yet been promoted to be the default. What follows is the design commentary on what the Fall students will face that the site does not yet support.

The Fall 160 journey, in one paragraph

Fall students design and run an experiment over the course of the quarter. The Week-1 journey we are designing first carries them through a sequence of methodological decisions: browse the corpus's open questions (the DYK browser); pick a topic to pursue; admit a candidate paper as either in-corpus (Path A) or uploaded (Path B, requiring the V7-Lite ingest); inspect the paper's evidence and warrant profile; decide whether to substitute a VR-tractable measure for the original paper's measure; consult the VOI panel for whether the substitution is worth the cost; check the design against Cook & Campbell's four validities; capture the deliverable. Nine surfaces, all wireframed in ka_week1_wireframe_2026-05-17.html. None of those nine surfaces is yet a live production page.

New demands on the site from the Fall design

The Fall journey requires the site to support several capabilities the current production version does not have. First, a DYK browser surface (Surface 2) that renders the corpus's most interesting findings as student-facing cards with explicit CTAs for Explore, Add to short-list, and Find related papers — depending on a fresh DYK generation pass over the corpus, which is what the AG V7-complete run on 800 papers will produce. Second, a per-topic page (Surface 3) that holds the student's short-list with persistent state across logins. Third, an evaluate-a-paper interface (Surface 4) with two paths: paper in corpus, immediate evaluation; paper not in corpus, student upload feeding the V7-Lite ingest pipeline which Codex is building. Fourth, a choose-a-measure interface (Surface 4b) that renders the substitution skill's ranked recommendation. Fifth, an inline VOI-panel component (Surface 5) embedded on Surfaces 2, 3, and 4 and reading from the AG V2 credence schema. Sixth, a VR-measurability source-of-truth page (Surface 6) explaining what VR can and cannot measure. Seventh, a methodological-pitfalls page (Surface 7) running about 5,000 words. Eighth, a Cook & Campbell four-validities showcase (Surface 7b) and its active self-check companion. Ninth, an exemplars page (Surface 8) and the deliverable-capture page (Surface 9). None of these is in the student-setup landing above; that landing is the Spring track-picker.

What is drafted in markdown and ready to render

Two of the nine Fall surfaces have their content fully drafted as source-of-truth markdown and are waiting on Codex to render them as live HTML pages. Surface 6 (VR measurability) lives at 160sp/ka_vr_measurability_content_2026-05-18.md; about 3,160 words covering four positive measurement families (head pose, IATs, EDA/HR/HRV, eye tracking) and five exclusions. Surface 7b (Cook & Campbell) lives at 160sp/ka_validities_showcase_content_2026-05-18.md; about 2,800 words covering all four validities plus a worked example on the ceiling-height contestation.

What is blocked or in flight

Surface 7 (methodological pitfalls) content drafting is blocked on VOI panel review. Surface 4 and 4b are blocked on Codex completing the V7-Lite ingest pipeline and the substitution skill, both specified in docs/CODEX_V7_LITE_BUILD_SPEC_2026-05-18.md and docs/CODEX_SUBSTITUTION_SKILL_BUILD_SPEC_2026-05-18.md. Surface 5 (VOI panel) needs the eight-panelist briefing letters drafted and sent. The DYK browser (Surface 2) needs the AG V7-complete pass to populate the corpus with the new card payload, per docs/AG_DYK_V7_PRODUCTION_REQUIREMENTS_2026-05-19.md. Surface 3's short-list persistence is its own Codex sprint. Surface 7b's self-check active companion exists as a stub at ka_validities_self_check.html with a "coming soon" notice.

What is misleading on this landing as you see it now

The hero copy and the section headings above refer to the Spring-style track structure (four tracks, eight-week schedule, A0 and A1 cross-track assignments). The Fall journey will not have four tracks in the same shape; it has a single design-and-run-an-experiment path with the nine-surface scaffolding. If the page above is to be the Fall entry, it needs to be rebuilt around the nine surfaces, not the tracks. The cleanest move is to leave this page as the Spring landing and to build a new ka_fall160_home.html (or to promote the wireframe surfaces into production pages and have a fresh Fall landing route to them). That choice belongs to the instructor; the design notes here flag it rather than presume it.

Open instructor decisions

First, whether the Fall entry should overwrite the current student-setup page or live at a new route while the existing one stays for Spring continuity. Second, the date by which the nine Fall surfaces need to be live and student-tested — the dependency graph from the May 18 sprint plan suggests mid-summer for the surfaces, but the calibration and small-cohort dry-run timeline is open. Third, whether the AG V7-complete run on 800 papers (using Gemini 2.5 API credits, per the authorisation flag in the DYK production-requirements document) should run before or after the surfaces are promoted; running first means the DYK browser has real material to render, running after means we can wire the surfaces against a smaller validation corpus first. Fourth, whether any of the Spring-style cross-track assignments (A0, A1, the tagging exercise) carry into Fall or are retired in favour of the design-and-run pipeline. Fifth, the panel-briefing letters for VOI — the simulated synthesis is done but the real panel has not been written to.

If something on this page looks broken

The page above is the existing Spring landing minus the noisy mid-page commentary the previous version of this section had wrongly inserted. The four navigation grids (sections 4, 5, 6, 7) are pure link blocks now — no status badges, no in-line prose, no "coming soon" labels. Anything that looks broken on this landing is a bug. Anything missing for the Fall journey is by design — the Fall surfaces are not yet promoted into production and should not appear on this landing until they are.
