Tracks

The four track-specific learning paths. Pick one in Week 1; you'll spend most of the quarter inside the track you chose.

Fall Week-1 Journey

The nine-surface goal-driven journey COGS 160 Fall students follow in Week 1 — find a topic, evaluate it for VR, commit a deliverable. These pages are built from the May-17 wireframe; they are page-complete and content-complete but not yet wired to the live corpus, so they are marked In progress.

Surface 1 — Week-1 Landing In progress

The hub. Three working modes plus supporting reading and the Week-1 exit criteria.

Surface 2 — Did-You-Know Browser In progress

Surprising findings as cards, filterable by topic family and VR-tractability.

Surface 3 — Per-Topic Page In progress

Meta-review, ranked anchor papers, a persistent short-list, and the commit action.

Surface 4 — Evaluate a Paper (admit-mode) In progress

Two paths: paper in corpus (immediate evaluation) or uploaded (V7-Lite ingest).

Surface 4b — Choose a Measure (choice-mode) In progress

Week-3 hand-off. Side-by-side measure comparison with a ranked recommendation.

Surface 6 — VR Measurability In progress

What VR can and cannot measure. Source-of-truth explainer; Surface 5 (VOI panel) is embedded inside Surfaces 2, 3, and 4.

Surface 7 — Methodological Pitfalls In progress

Threats to anticipate and standards a short project can meet.

Surface 7b — Cook & Campbell's Four Validities In progress

The canonical validity taxonomy with a contested-finding worked example.

Surface 8 — Exemplary VR Experiments In progress

Eight role-model studies, each with the feature worth imitating.

Surface 9 — Week-1 Deliverable In progress

Deliverable capture: topic, rationale, bibliography, and the pre-submission self-check.
