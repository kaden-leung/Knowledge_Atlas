# CNFA Gold Standard — Evaluation Corpus

**Author:** Kaden Leung
**Date:** 2026-06-02
**Purpose:** 30-paper canonical bibliography used as the evaluation corpus for retrieval recall, error taxonomy, and query ablation analysis. Used in TRACK2_EVALUATION_REPORT.md.

## Selection criteria

Papers were selected to represent breadth across the CNFA field:
- Core empirical CNFA studies (EEG, fMRI, cortisol, wayfinding)
- Foundational environmental psychology theories (ART, SRT)
- Architectural neuroscience reviews and frameworks
- Key mechanistic neuroscience underlying CNFA frameworks (predictive coding, embodied cognition)
- Built environment × health and wellbeing

Papers outside this set — particularly adjacent engineering or education papers — were excluded even when they mention architecture.

## Relevance tags

- `core_CNFA` — paper studies how built environment directly affects human neural, physiological, or psychological processes
- `cnfa_adjacent` — paper addresses built environment and wellbeing/health from a broader perspective (not neuroscience-specific)
- `theoretical_foundation` — mechanistic or theoretical paper that underlies CNFA frameworks without studying architecture directly

## Coverage notes

The 30 papers intentionally cover all 10 COGS-160 Atlas frameworks and all three primary CNFA research traditions:
1. **Neuroscience-first** (Kok, Clark, Corbetta) — mechanistic underpinning
2. **Environmental psychology** (Kaplan, Ulrich, Gifford) — established empirical tradition
3. **Architectural neuroscience / neuroarchitecture** (Djebbara, Vartanian, Fich, Gramann) — direct CNFA studies

Gaps (deliberately included to test retrieval failures):
- Several papers are books (no DOI) — Sternberg, Eberhard, Ellard, Browning, Golledge, Damasio — expected to be `never-retrieved`
- Kok 2012 is pure visual neuroscience with no architectural vocabulary — expected to fail the keyword classifier
- Heschong and Browning are grey literature / reports — expected to fail abstract collection
